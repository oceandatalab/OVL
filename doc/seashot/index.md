# The SEAShot tool

<a href="https://www.oceandatalab.com"><img src="../../images/odl-logo.svg" alt="OceanDataLab" width="150" align="right"></a>

SEAShot, available at <https://seashot.odl.bzh>, is used to turn a view into a
shareable story with a preview image. It is one of the service proposed to share a test case using the [OVL portal](../ovl/index.md).

## In this chapter

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **[Sign up / Log in](login.md)** | Why an account is needed, and where to create one. |
| **[Creating a seashot](create-seashot.md)** | Capturing a view from the portal, describing it, and turning it into an animation. |
| **[Manage and share your seashots](manage-seashots.md)** | Publishing as private, unlisted or public, and sharing the result. |

## Asking a question or reporting a bug

Open an [issue](https://github.com/oceandatalab/OVL/issues) — for questions about
using the tools as well as for bugs. When reporting a bug, please say which tool
you were using, what you expected, and what happened instead.

You can also reach us at <https://www.oceandatalab.com>.
