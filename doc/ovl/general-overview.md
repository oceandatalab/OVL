# General overview

OVL puts co-located satellite, model and in-situ ocean data on an interactive
map, with a timeline to move through them and tools to draw, measure and
extract. The video below is a short tour of the portal — the map, the data
panel, the timeline and the drawing tools — before the rest of this chapter
covers each of them in detail.

## Glossary

Terms used throughout this chapter:

* **Base map** — the background map drawn under your data, such as EMODnet
  World or one of the Google maps. Chosen with the globe button in the top
  toolbar.
* **Layer** — a dataset you activated, drawn on the map. Also called a
  **product** when referring to it in the catalogue or the Display data panel.
* **Granule** — a single file of data, covering one acquisition and time
  range, that makes up a layer.
* **Display data panel** — the panel, opened from the top toolbar, listing the
  layers you selected and the controls to reorder, filter or style them.
* **Timespan** — the width of the time window data is searched for in, set
  with the buttons at the left end of the timeline (1-Hour, 12-Hour, Daily,
  and so on).
* **Collocation** — matching the selected products in time and space, so the
  timeline shows only the dates where they overlap.
* **Annotation** — a shape you draw on the map yourself — a point, arrow,
  polyline, polygon, eddy, mushroom or text — stored in your browser and
  editable or exportable at any time.
* **Transect** — a polyline drawn to extract the values of the layers along
  it.
* **Hotspot** — a bookmarked case study you can jump to directly.
* **Syntool** — the open-source viewer, developed by OceanDataLab, that powers
  the OVL portal.
