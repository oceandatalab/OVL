# The OVL portal

<a href="https://ovl.oceandatalab.com"><img src="../../images/ovl-logo.svg" alt="OVL" width="90" align="right"></a>

The Ocean Virtual Laboratory (OVL) is a web portal that lets oceanographers
discover various co-located remote sensing ocean
observation  covering a region of interest — alongside model outputs and
in-situ measurements. It runs in a browser at <https://ovl.oceandatalab.com> and
requires no installation.

The portal is powered by Syntool, the open-source viewer developed by
OceanDataLab, and is partly funded by the European Space Agency.
